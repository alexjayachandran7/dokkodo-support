# Musashi's Dokkōdō
**The 21 Precepts of the Samurai**
A minimal iOS app presenting Miyamoto Musashi's *Dokkōdō* — written in 1645, one week before his death — in both English and Japanese.

# About
The *Dokkōdō* ("The Way of Walking Alone") is Musashi's final work: 21 austere precepts distilled from a lifetime of mastery, discipline, and solitude. This app presents them without distraction — no notifications, no streaks, no gamification.
